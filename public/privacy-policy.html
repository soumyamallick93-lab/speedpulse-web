Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how KOSHLIVA LABS operates the SpeedPulse broadband utility located at speedpulse.site.

1. Data Minimization & Local Execution

All metric computations—including throughput speeds, latency calculations, and jitter analysis—are processed locally in your browser memory. We do not store or log client IP addresses or personal usage telemetry.

2. Network Metadata & IP Detection

To identify your Internet Service Provider and closest edge routing point, SpeedPulse makes transient client-side requests to public geo-IP services. This data is displayed in your active browser session only.

3. Cookies and Advertising

SpeedPulse uses Google AdSense and third-party advertising cookies to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this policy, contact our compliance team at support@speedpulse.site or visit our Contact Page.
