About KOSHLIVA LABS

KOSHLIVA LABS is an independent software research and digital utility initiative dedicated to our founding mantra: Building Technology for Everyone.

Our Mission

SpeedPulse was created to provide consumers, developers, and gamers with transparent, unthrottled network verification directly in the browser without bloatware or heavy advertisements.

Technology & Architecture

SpeedPulse bypasses typical ISP burst-buffer tricks by utilizing concurrent multi-stream sockets connected directly to distributed global edge nodes.
