How Internet Speed Testing Works

A reliable speed test evaluates four core network metrics:

  • Download Speed (Downlink): Determines the maximum rate at which your connection fetches data packets from nearest edge content delivery nodes. Expressed in Megabits per second (Mbps).
  • Upload Speed (Uplink): Measures the rate at which your device transfers data back to global servers, crucial for live streaming, cloud backups, and video conferencing.
  • Ping Latency (ms): The round-trip transmission time required for a packet to travel from your hardware to an edge server and return. Lower ping indicates a faster response.
  • Jitter (ms): The variance of latency over consecutive packet transmissions. Low jitter ensures seamless esports gameplay in competitive gaming titles.

Features of the 1TB Download Stress Tester
