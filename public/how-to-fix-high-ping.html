How to Fix High Ping & Eliminate Jitter: The Comprehensive Engineering Guide

Published by KOSHLIVA LABS Engineering Team

High ping and inconsistent jitter degrade online competitive gaming and cause packet loss. Bandwidth and network latency are fundamentally different: upgrading speed does not always lower ping.

Key Takeaway: Bandwidth is pipe capacity; latency is transmission speed. Bufferbloat in home routers is the most common cause of sudden spikes.

1. Diagnose and Eliminate Bufferbloat

Enable Smart Queue Management (SQM) or CAKE algorithms on your router to prioritize gaming packets over bulk file transfers.

2. Use Direct Ethernet Instead of 2.4GHz Wi-Fi

Switching to a physical Cat6 cable drops first-hop wireless interference and local latency to sub-1ms.

3. Switch to Low-Latency DNS Resolvers

Set primary DNS to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare) or 8.8.8.8 (Google) to reduce route lookup times.
